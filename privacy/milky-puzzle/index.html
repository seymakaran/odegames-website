PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy is prepared by ODEgames and applies specifically to the Milky Puzzle mobile application published on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

Protecting children's privacy is extremely important to us. Milky Puzzle is designed for children, and we are committed to complying with applicable children's privacy laws and platform requirements.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

ODEgames does not collect, request, or store personal information from children or parents through Milky Puzzle.

The app does not request:

  • Names or nicknames
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise or approximate location data
  • Photos, videos, or audio recordings
  • User accounts or login credentials

No registration, login, or account creation is required to use Milky Puzzle.

1.2 Analytics and Diagnostic Data

Milky Puzzle does not use analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking services to track users or their activity.

ODEgames does not use the app to collect analytics or diagnostic information about children.

The app does not currently use:

  • Analytics or behavioral tracking tools
  • Advertising SDKs or advertising networks
  • Third-party tracking technologies
  • Crash reporting services operated by ODEgames
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

2. Advertising

Milky Puzzle does not display advertisements of any kind.

There are:

  • No banner ads
  • No interstitial ads
  • No rewarded ads
  • No third-party advertising networks

3. In-App Purchases

Milky Puzzle offers a single, one-time in-app purchase that permanently unlocks the app's additional content and features.

  • No subscriptions
  • No recurring payments
  • No time-limited access

Purchases are processed through the platform on which the app was obtained:

  • Google Play for Android devices
  • Apple App Store for iOS devices

ODEgames does not collect or store payment card, bank account, or billing information. Payment processing is handled by Google or Apple in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

4. Third-Party Services

Milky Puzzle uses platform services provided by Google Play and Apple for in-app purchase processing and related store functionality. These services may process information independently in accordance with their own privacy policies.

ODEgames does not use third-party advertising or analytics services within Milky Puzzle.

5. Data Sharing

ODEgames does not sell, rent, or share children's personal information with advertisers or other third parties.

Information necessary to process an in-app purchase is handled by Google Play or Apple App Store as part of their respective payment and store services.

6. Children's Privacy

Milky Puzzle is intended for children. We design and operate the app with children's privacy in mind and seek to comply with applicable requirements, including:

  • Google Play Families policies
  • Apple App Store Kids Category requirements
  • COPPA (Children's Online Privacy Protection Act), where applicable

ODEgames does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Milky Puzzle.

7. Data Security

ODEgames does not directly collect or store personal information from Milky Puzzle users. We also aim to minimize the use of external services and data processing within the app, particularly because the app is intended for children.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Milky Puzzle, legal requirements, or platform policies.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
