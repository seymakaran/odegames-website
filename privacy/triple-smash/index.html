Triple Smash – Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy is prepared by ODEgames and applies to the Triple Smash mobile game available on Google Play and the Apple App Store.

1. Information We Collect

Triple Smash does not require users to create an account or provide information such as their name, email address, phone number, or login credentials in order to play the game.

However, limited technical, diagnostic, gameplay, and purchase-related information may be processed as described below.

1.1 Gameplay and Analytics Data

Triple Smash may collect limited gameplay and analytics information to help us understand how the game is used and to improve the gameplay experience.

This information may include:

  • Level starts, completions, and failures
  • Level progress and number of attempts
  • Gameplay duration and session information
  • Use of in-game features and power-ups
  • Interactions with in-app purchase features
  • Game version and basic technical information

We use this information to understand game performance, identify levels or features that may need improvement, balance gameplay, and improve the overall player experience.

1.2 Crash and Diagnostic Data

Triple Smash may process crash and diagnostic information to detect, investigate, and fix technical problems.

This may include information such as:

  • Crash reports and error information
  • Game and application version
  • Operating system and device-related technical information
  • Performance and diagnostic information

This information is used for troubleshooting, improving stability, fixing bugs, and improving game performance.

2. Advertising

Triple Smash does not display advertisements.

We do not use advertising networks for targeted advertising within Triple Smash.

3. In-App Purchases

Triple Smash offers optional in-app purchases. These purchases include consumable virtual coins used within the game.

Purchases are processed through the platform from which the game was obtained:

  • Google Play for Android devices
  • Apple App Store for iOS devices

ODEgames does not receive or store users' full credit card or bank account information. Payment processing is handled by Google or Apple in accordance with their respective terms and privacy policies.

Google Play or Apple may provide limited information related to a purchase as necessary to process, verify, or provide purchased content.

4. Third-Party Services

Triple Smash uses platform and technology services provided by third parties for certain functions.

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play on Android devices and the Apple App Store on iOS devices.

Triple Smash may also use third-party services for gameplay analytics, crash reporting, diagnostics, and application distribution. These services may process limited technical or usage information as necessary to provide their functionality.

Triple Smash does not use third-party advertising networks for targeted advertising.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed through Triple Smash may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain the game
  • Understand gameplay and player interactions
  • Improve level design and game balance
  • Identify and fix crashes and technical problems
  • Improve game stability and performance
  • Process in-app purchases
  • Prevent abuse and maintain game integrity

6. Data Sharing

ODEgames does not sell users' personal information.

Limited information may be processed by service providers used to operate Triple Smash, such as Google, Apple, and providers of analytics or diagnostic services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the security and integrity of the game and its users.

7. Children

Triple Smash is a general-audience game and is not specifically designed or directed toward children.

The game does not require children or other users to directly submit personal information such as their name, email address, or phone number to play.

8. Data Security

ODEgames takes reasonable measures to protect information processed in connection with Triple Smash and aims to limit data processing to information reasonably necessary to operate, maintain, and improve the game.

9. Data Retention

Information processed for analytics and diagnostic purposes is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy or as required by applicable law.

Retention periods may also depend on the services used to provide analytics, diagnostics, and platform functionality.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Triple Smash, the services used by the game, or applicable legal and platform requirements.

Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
